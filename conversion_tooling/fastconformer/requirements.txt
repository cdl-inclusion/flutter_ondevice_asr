# Installing Nemo and all its dependencies can be problematic on Mac.
# Below tested and works on Mac with Python 3.11.

# NeMo 2.3.0 HARD-pins these two (numpy<2.0.0, protobuf==4.24.4). Without them pip drifts to
# the modern onnx/onnxscript stack, which drags in numpy 2.x + protobuf 7.x and breaks NeMo
# and numba. Keeping these pins forces the older, mutually-compatible onnx-stack resolution.

nemo_toolkit[asr]==2.3.0
numpy<2
protobuf==4.24.4
librosa
soundfile
onnx
onnxruntime
onnxscript
